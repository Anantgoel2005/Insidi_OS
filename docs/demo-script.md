# Five-minute Insidi_OS demo

1. Sign in to Insidi_OS and open **Live Showcase**. Explain the three feeds: attacker activity, real detection findings, and synthetic sandbox responses.
2. Select employee data misuse at the four-second showcase pace. Click **Start live demo** and **Presenter view**.
3. Contrast the benign login with out-of-scope decoy access. Pause to explain the evidence and risk score.
4. Resume through bulk collection and an unapproved transfer. Point out that sandbox containment denies the next retry. No real data leaves the machine.
5. Open the investigation to show persisted events and alerts. Return to the showcase, change to administrator abuse, and contrast approved maintenance with audit evasion, privilege abuse and decoy credential export.
6. Close with the trust boundary: detection and alert records are real; attacks and containment are synthetic. Enterprise enforcement still requires trusted collectors and approved operational integrations.

For portfolio screenshots, capture the branded login, live stage after containment, and investigation evidence. Do not include real public IP addresses, API keys, or passwords. See the [live showcase guide](live-showcase.md) and [brand guide](brand.md) for presentation details.
